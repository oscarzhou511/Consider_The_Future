About the founder

Hello there! I'm Oscar Zhou and I'm filled with wonder by technology constantly. However, I am deeply concerned about humanity's future as we race to build the most powerful technology in human history. I founded this campaign to raise awareness of the existential and environmental risks of unconstrained AI development, and to demand that governments and corporations prioritise human safety over profit.

A little about me: I represented Australia at the International Olympiad for Artificial Intelligence in Bulgaria in 2024. I captained the Australian Senior Team at the Robotics for Good — Youth Challenge in 2026, and spoke at the United Nations AI for Good summit as part of the UNICEF Digital Impact initiative, engaging directly with world leaders across governments and industry. I'm also featured in The God We Made by Anna Goldsworthy.

Who pays the price?

Communities living near data centres face depleted water supplies, rising electricity bills, and constant industrial noise. Young graduates face career uncertainty as entire fields are automated overnight. Rural towns subsidise infrastructure they never asked for.

Frontier tech companies spent record millions on lobbying. In December 2025, an executive order stripped individual US states of the power to regulate AI — centralising control and silencing local democratic oversight.

What you can do

Talk about it.

Most people have a superficial understanding of artificial intelligence and do not understand the existential and environmental threats its development brings. Start the conversation at school, at home, at work. Change cannot happen without people caring about an issue.

Vote for it.

Voice your concerns to local politicians. Make informed decisions at elections. Support candidates who will put human safety ahead of corporate donations. Politicians have the power to apply direct pressure to AI companies and issue real legislation.

Demand accountability.

We need a global, independent organisation — like those governing nuclear technology — to audit AI progress, advise governments, and hold nations accountable. Mandatory safety audits before deployment. Protection for whistleblowers. No more self-regulation.

People already fighting for this

Advocacy groups like Siliconversations are pioneering a new model for technology campaigning — using sharp humour and accessible media to mobilise public attention. Working alongside watchdogs like ControlAI and the Future of Life Institute, they back viral reach with rigorous research.
